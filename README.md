# Publications

This repository lists peer-reviewed publications on which I am a coauthor in the
areas of Alzheimer’s disease and neuroimaging.

With the exception of the paper explicitly noted below, my authorship on these
publications reflects **shared contributions to structural MRI data processing
and infrastructure** that supported multiple downstream studies, rather than
direct involvement in each individual analysis.

---

## Author Profile:## 
Google Scholar → [https://scholar.google.com/citations](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=dlhfY5wAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao)

## Authorship Context

My contributions to the research program underlying these publications focused on:

- Structural MRI data preprocessing and quality control
- Maintenance and validation of shared imaging datasets
- Support of standardized data pipelines used across projects

This work enabled study-specific teams to conduct analyses across multiple
publications.

Unless explicitly stated, I did **not**:
- design study hypotheses or endpoints
- perform statistical modeling or inference
- lead result interpretation or manuscript writing
